Sources and methodology

These 100 game names come from the Game Maker's Toolkit written article, including titles mentioned briefly. This is an independent compilation with no affiliation to the author. The list does not measure the success rate of all Jam entries.

Steam links and release status were checked on Sep 26, 2026. Ninety-nine titles have a clear matching store page. “Loaded Dice” shares its name with games by different creators, so no link is given yet. Statuses may change. Genres are editorial primary labels, not official Steam categories; Jam years appear only when supported by a reliable source.

Game summaries were prepared from the source article and official Steam descriptions. Covers load from official Steam image URLs; the text remains readable without them.
